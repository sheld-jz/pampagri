Candaba, Pampanga

Livestock & Poultry Population in Candaba

Candaba is best known for its duck farming — it's the top duck egg producer in Pampanga, with a laying duck population of around 600,000. The town also raises other livestock on a smaller scale.

Egg Production

Candaba Ranks 3rd in Egg Production in Central Luzon

According to the Philippine Statistics Association, Candaba ranks third among all Central Luzon provinces in egg production. The town's vast duck farms drive this output, supported by access to rice paddies where ducks forage naturally.

Climate Risk

Pampanga Logs ₱10M in Livestock Damages from Typhoon

The livestock industry in Pampanga is highly vulnerable to flooding. During Typhoon Carina in 2024, Candaba alone recorded ₱7.4 million in damages to egg production — the highest in the entire province — highlighting the need for flood-resilient farming practices.
